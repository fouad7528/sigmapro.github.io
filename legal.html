Your data files and the analysis are processed in your browser. We do not upload or store your data files.

  • Downloads: a record of each report download (type, date) to apply your plan.
  • Licence: a random device ID stored in your browser plus a coarse computer signature (platform, number of processor cores, time zone, screen size) to enforce the one-computer rule.
